A good way to get started

  1. Pick your course in the sidebar so Browse, Exam and Match use the right content.
  2. Study every day — SM-2 spaced repetition schedules your cards so you review what you're about to forget.
  3. Use Weak Words to close your personal knowledge gaps.
  4. Finish with the Exam or Recall Exam (pass = 85%) to see if you're ready.
  5. Learn commands in the CLI Lab and check symptoms in Diagnosis before the lab exam.

Progress is saved in your browser (localStorage). Switch courses any time — your SM-2 history is kept per card, not per course.

How the CLI Lab works
  • Read the prompt (scenario) and type the command, then press Enter to check it.
  • Stuck? Press Show Answer to reveal the command and its explanation.
  • Navigate with Prev / Next, or Shuffle to randomize the deck.
  • Study Mode shows prompt + answer + explanation without typing — good for memorizing.
  • All Commands opens a searchable list of every command in the deck.
